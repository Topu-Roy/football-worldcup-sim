# Keywords — Marketing & SEO

Product: single-player browser web game — draft a fantasy World Cup XI from historical squads (1970–2026), run through a simulated group stage + knockout tournament with penalty shootouts.

Markets: global (football terms) + US (soccer terms), English only.

Volumes are global monthly estimates from Google Ads data (TheSEOLabs), relative basis — exact numbers vary 30–50% by tool. Priority P1 = product core, P2 = feature/seasonal, P3 = content SEO, P4 = US market / viral.
